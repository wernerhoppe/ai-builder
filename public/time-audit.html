Rank #1 — Quick Win · Est. 1–2 hrs to build
📧 My inbox processes itself — I open Gmail and see only the 3 messages that actually need me, not 30.
What's happening today
14+ emails/day arrive (newsletters, LinkedIn pings, bank alerts, promotions). You spend ~15–20 min/day just triaging — even when 95% of it is noise. Your 3 actual sent emails this month were buried under 400+ received.
What the automated version looks like

Quick Win ~1.5 hrs reclaimed/week Gmail MCP already connected
Rank #2 — Quick Win · Est. 1 hr to build
📚 My study flashcards are ready before I sit down — generated automatically from my class notes and textbook readings.
What's happening today
Calendar shows 5+ explicit "flashcard" sessions (FIN 201, ECON midterms). You're manually creating or sourcing cards before studying. Multiple calendar blocks show "flashcards + practice test" = card-making is baked into the study block itself, stealing ~30–45 min of actual study time per session.
What the automated version looks like
When you upload a lecture slide PDF or paste notes, Claude generates a complete Anki-formatted flashcard deck in <2 minutes. You can also paste a textbook chapter URL and get a structured Q&A set. Triggered by: uploading study material. Output: ready-to-import deck or printable sheet.
Quick Win ~2 hrs reclaimed/week during school Directly supports 4.0 goal
