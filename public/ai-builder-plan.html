Build Roadmap

What I'm
building

Five automations ranked by impact: hours reclaimed × alignment with the top goal. Highest first.

#1 — Highest Impact
Quick Win · <2 hrs 1.5 hrs/week reclaimed
Not started
My inbox processes itself — I open Gmail and see only the 3 messages that actually need me, not 30.
What's happening today
14+ emails arrive daily: 6 newsletters, 8+ LinkedIn pings, bank alerts, promotions. I spend 15–20 min/day triaging — even when 95% is noise. My 3 real sent emails this month were buried under 400+ received. The inbox is a one-way flood.
What the automated version looks like
Gmail filters auto-label and archive newsletters and LinkedIn notifications. Claude scans new email daily, surfaces only messages requiring a response, and drafts replies for the 1–2 that need one. Optional: a daily 3-line digest of anything worth knowing.
Quick Win Gmail MCP already connected Immediate — build this week
#2 — Highest PACS Impact
Medium Lift · ~4 hrs 30+ hrs/week reclaimed at PACS
Not started
I walk into the PACS summer internship with a working SNF referral parser — not a pitch deck about building one.
What's happening today
PACS admissions staff manually parse 50+ page hospital referral PDFs — diagnosis, insurance, care needs, red flags. Each one takes 30–60 minutes. Zero PACS-specific build blocks in my current time data. The tool is fully buildable with Claude today, but hasn't been started.
What the automated version looks like
Admissions uploads a referral PDF. Claude extracts diagnosis, insurance type, care needs, red flags, and response priority. Output: a 1-page structured summary ready in under 60 seconds. Building this now means walking into June with a live demo, not a slide.
Medium Lift Highest PACS alignment Build before summer
#3 — Direct GPA Impact
Quick Win · <1 hr 2 hrs/week reclaimed (school)
Not started
My flashcard decks are ready before I sit down — generated in 2 minutes from whatever I'm studying, not built during the study block itself.
What's happening today
Calendar had 5+ explicit "flashcard" sessions: "flash cards lunch," "FIN 201 flashcards + final outline," "Econ cumulative portion review (flashcards and practice tests)." Card-making is baked into study blocks, stealing 30–45 min of actual study time per session.
What the automated version looks like
Upload a lecture PDF or paste notes → Claude generates a complete, Anki-formatted Q&A deck in under 2 minutes. Also works from textbook chapter URLs. Output: ready-to-import deck or a printable study sheet. Triggered by uploading any study material.
Quick Win Directly supports 4.0 goal Most useful next semester
#4 — Attention Recovery
Medium Lift · ~3 hrs 1 hr/week reclaimed
Not started
I spend 5 minutes on Friday reviewing one AI-curated brief — not an hour grazing six newsletters and LinkedIn.
What's happening today
Six newsletters daily (Morning Brew, Finimize, Investopedia ×2, Robinhood Snacks, Healthcare Brew) + LinkedIn job alerts + Coinbase/Robinhood pings = ~30 emails/day of ambient noise. Signal I actually want is buried in promotional content.
What the automated version looks like
Scheduled Claude agent reads inbox every Friday morning, synthesizes a 5-bullet weekly digest filtered for: (1) healthcare/SNF relevance, (2) AI tools, (3) finance tied to coursework. Delivered as one email. All original newsletters auto-archived after processing.
Medium Lift Gmail MCP already connected Pair with #1 inbox cleanup
#5 — Annual Time Recovery
Quick Win · <2 hrs ~10 hrs reclaimed/year
Not started
Tax season takes 2 hours in February instead of 12 hours during finals week — every document is organized when I need it.
What's happening today
~12 hours of reactive tax work hit during finals week: TaxDome (59 visits), IRS.gov (33 visits), recovering a prior employer's payroll login (4 emails, account locked), CPA back-and-forth. Undocumented, unplanned, and collision-timed with FIN 201 and ECON 110 finals.
What the automated version looks like
A scheduled Claude agent fires every February 1st, emails a checklist of every document needed (W-2s, 1099s, prior-year returns, business expenses, login credentials) with the correct portal for each. Takes 20 min to set up once. No firefighting during finals. Ever again.
Quick Win Set-and-forget annual trigger Prevents finals-week collisions
Total estimated weekly reclaim: ~5–6 hours. The biggest single lever isn't calendar optimization (zero meetings to cut) — it's cleaning up the information diet (#1, #4) and turning AI skills toward the actual PACS use cases (#2) before summer starts.
